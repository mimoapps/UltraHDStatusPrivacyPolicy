Ultra HD Status

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

Developer: Kepler Labs  ·  Contact: ajithmemana@outlook.com

Ultra HD Status ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") is an Android application that shrinks video files on your device and helps you share them, including to WhatsApp. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

At a glance

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools of any kind.
  • We do not sell your information, and we never use it for advertising.
  • The App only ever sees the specific video(s) you choose to share — never your full photo or video library.
  • If you use the optional "send via WhatsApp code" feature, your video is stored on our servers only temporarily and is automatically deleted within one hour, whether or not it was ever picked up.
  • No personal information is required to shrink a video or share it directly to WhatsApp from your device.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 The videos you choose to share

When you pick a video in the App, you do so through Android's own picker screen — the App never gets open-ended access to your photos or videos, only the file(s) you personally select.

  • Shrinking the video happens entirely on your device. The video is not sent anywhere during this step.
  • Sharing directly to WhatsApp (when WhatsApp is installed on your phone) hands the shrunk video straight to the WhatsApp app using Android's normal "share" function. The video does not pass through our servers in this flow.
  • Sharing through a WhatsApp code (an alternate way to send larger or higher-quality videos) briefly uploads the shrunk video to our secure cloud storage under a random one-time code. You then send that code as a WhatsApp message to our business number, and our system replies with the matching video.
Any video uploaded this way is automatically and permanently deleted from our servers within one hour of being uploaded — regardless of whether it was ever collected. We do not keep long-term copies, and we do not look at, analyze, or otherwise use the contents of your videos.

1.2 Information we receive if you message us on WhatsApp

If you use the WhatsApp-code feature described above, WhatsApp (operated by Meta) shares with us the message you sent (the code) and your WhatsApp phone number, so we know which video to send back and where to send it. This information is used only to fulfill that one request — we do not use it for marketing, and we never message you first.

1.3 Settings stored on your device

The App remembers a few simple preferences (such as whether a haptic buzz is turned on) directly on your phone. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us; it is removed automatically if you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

1.4 Information we do NOT collect

We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools in the App. We do not collect your precise location, contacts, browsing history, or any device advertising identifiers, and we do not require an account, email address, or password to use the App.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited information described above only to:

  • Shrink and prepare the videos you select;
  • Deliver a video back to you when you use the WhatsApp-code feature;
  • Keep the App and its backend running reliably and securely (for example, basic technical logs used to catch errors or abuse); and
  • Respond to you if you contact us.

We do not use your information for advertising, to build a profile about you, or for any purpose unrelated to the features above.

4. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own advertising or marketing purposes. We use a small number of trusted service providers strictly to operate the App:

  • Google Firebase (Google Cloud Platform) — provides the temporary cloud storage and backend processing used only by the optional WhatsApp-code feature. Google processes this data on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (WhatsApp Business Platform) — used only if you message our WhatsApp business number, so we can deliver the requested video back to you over WhatsApp. Meta's handling of that message is governed by its own policy. See WhatsApp's Privacy Policy.

Basic technical logs generated by these services (for example, timestamps or error codes used to diagnose problems or prevent abuse) may be kept for a short standard period — typically no more than 30 days — before being automatically deleted. These logs do not include the contents of your videos.

5. Data Retention

  • Videos uploaded through the WhatsApp-code feature are deleted automatically and permanently within one hour of upload, whether or not they were ever retrieved.
  • Device settings remain on your phone until you clear the App's data or uninstall it.
  • Technical logs, if any, are kept only briefly (see Section 4) and are not used to identify you personally.

We do not otherwise retain your video content or messages on our servers.

6. International Data Transfers

Our cloud service providers may process information on servers located outside your own country, including in the United States. By using the App's WhatsApp-code feature, you understand that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in a country other than your own, which may have different data protection laws than your country of residence.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect the limited information the App handles, including encrypting data in transit and automatically deleting uploaded videos within an hour. That said, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights such as the ability to:

  • Ask what information we hold that relates to you;
  • Ask us to correct or delete that information;
  • Object to, or ask us to restrict, certain uses of your information; and
  • Receive a copy of your information in a portable format.

Because uploaded videos are deleted automatically within an hour and we don't keep accounts or profiles, in most cases there is simply no ongoing data to access or delete by the time a request reaches us. You can still reach out with any privacy question or request at the contact details below, and we will respond within a reasonable time (generally within 30 days). We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is nothing to "opt out" of in that respect.

You can also avoid the WhatsApp-code feature entirely and instead share a shrunk video directly from your device using Android's normal share function, in which case nothing is uploaded to our servers at all.
